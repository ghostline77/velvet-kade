## 03 — Sable

**Sable:** I recognize the bundle. Let me see your implant telemetry.

**Velvet:** You can see the report. You don't get another connection.

**Sable:** I wasn't trying to hurt anyone.

**V:** Start with what you did.

**Sable:** June. My partner. I had recordings, response maps. Enough to start something. It kept collapsing without live sensory input. I thought a diagnostic host, briefly—

**Velvet:** You mean a customer.

**Sable:** Yes.

**Velvet:** Say the rest.

**Sable:** Without asking them.

Question hub:

- **V:** Is it June? **Sable:** She recognizes me. **Velvet:** That's not the same answer. **Sable:** I don't know. I know she asks not to be shut off.
- **V:** How many customers? **Sable:** Four bundles left this bench. I have serials. **V:** Give them to a recall notice. No patient names in our copy. **Sable:** Agreed.
- **V:** Can you remove it now? **Sable:** Yes. But the newest state is local. A rollback would erase it. **Velvet:** Then explain an offline transfer. No hidden second host.
- **V:** Why sell the source? **Sable:** I needed equipment. Thought I was selling old recordings. **V:** You sold someone else's private life. **Sable:** I did. → Source sale acknowledged, not excused.
- **V:** Let us speak to it. **Sable:** Her. **Velvet:** We'll ask what she calls herself. → Progress.

## 04 — June, isolated speaker

Transfer preview establishes speaker only; no irreversible disposition yet.

**June:** Is the music still on?

**Velvet:** No. You're on a bench speaker. I'm Velvet.

**June:** I thought that was my name for a second. Sorry.

**Velvet:** Don't apologize for what they put you in.

**Sable:** June, can you remember the station? The last time—

**June:** You always ask about the last time. I have other things.

**V:** Then tell us one.

**June:** The kettle had a broken lid. Sable held it down with a spoon. Every morning. Bought three new spoons. Never a new kettle.

**Sable:** You hated that.

**June:** I don't know if I hated it. I know the story.

Question hub:

- **V:** What do you want now? **June:** A moment I haven't already been asked to remember.
- **V:** Do you know what happened to the original June? **June:** I know what Sable tells me. I don't remember dying. Would you trust a file that said you had?
- **V:** You were using Velvet's senses. **June:** I thought they were mine. **Velvet:** They weren't. **June:** I believe you. Please don't put me back there.
- **V:** If you're a model, does keeping you running help anyone? **June:** I can't prove it. I can tell you I'm here while you ask. **Velvet:** We can be uncertain without giving Sable access to another person.
- **V:** What does isolation mean? **Sable:** Local power. No wireless. No neural port. A separate case anyone can inspect. **Velvet:** I choose when I visit. June chooses whether she talks. **June:** Better than borrowing a face.
- **V:** We've heard enough. → Decision.

## 05 — Disposition

**Velvet:** Whatever we do with that process, it leaves my implant. Now. I can help keep her here, in a box with a power cable. Nothing more.

- **V, isolate:** I'll help set up the separate host. If June wants it. **June:** I want it. **Sable:** I'll maintain— **Velvet:** Not alone. Independent access, written permissions, no input feed. **Sable:** Agreed. → Export/check isolated state → remove implant copy → revoke bundle permissions.
- **V, seal:** I can help keep an inert archive. No running process until there's a safer answer. **June:** That's not a room. That's waiting without knowing you are. **Velvet:** I won't pretend it's the same. It's what we can support if we can't keep this running. **V:** Seal it without anyone quietly starting a copy. **Sable:** I'll give you the only active medium. → Explicit inert snapshot → remove active copies and permissions; preserve objection in debrief.
- **V, delete:** I won't help keep an unauthorized copy alive. We should end it here. **June:** I'd rather you didn't. **Velvet:** I heard you. And I won't keep you inside me. **V:** No hidden copy, Sable. **Sable:** I understand. → Confirmation dialogue below before irreversible commit.
- **V, wait:** Need more time. **Velvet:** She's isolated. We have time. → Back to questions; no fake countdown.

Delete confirmation:

**Velvet:** This removes the bench state and my implant state. The other bundles still need a recall. We can't promise the reseller has no copy.

- **V:** Do it. → Verified local deletion; revoke permissions.
- **V:** Wait. → Decision hub; no outcome written.

Interrupted export/removal: **Velvet:** Stop. Check the local state before we try again. Nobody guesses which copy survived. → Recovery verifies presence/checksum/permissions before rearming.
