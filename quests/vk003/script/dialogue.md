## 05 — Evidence and decision

Mandatory readable documents, accessible without a cyberdeck through pass or native maintenance interaction:

**Billing rules:** Retention uplift: prompt host with recent loss keywords when departure probability exceeds 0.65. Offer credit before customer reaches exit. Sales staff must not describe prediction-assisted prompts as automated.

**Data policy:** Imported contact fragments may be obtained through third-party identity brokers. Guest withdrawal stops future messages; previously ingested relationship features remain available to the model. Internal instruction: do not expose this distinction in guest controls.

**Retention schedule:** Indefinite feature retention pending product sunset. Maintenance bundle vendor: RHEE / neural interface diagnostics. Operator credential revocation stops new imports. Active targeting controller has local disconnect.

After documents read:

**V:** They don't need someone to love the host. Just to believe leaving would lose them something.

**Velvet:** Live feed first. I'm cutting that now.

Disconnect succeeds before archive choice; interrupted access retries without falsely showing shutdown.

**Io, if contacted:** Tell me what you're taking. I need to know whether I'm running tonight.

**Velvet:** Rules, payments, signatures. No guest names. No staff credential that points at you.

- **V, ask:** Why not dump everything? **Velvet:** Because “evidence” doesn't make a stranger's messages ours. → Return to decision.
- **V, ask:** Can they rebuild? **Velvet:** Yes. We can wreck this copy or give people proof of what it did. Neither buys a perfect city. → Return.
- **V, destroy:** Kill the model and profiles. **Velvet:** Keep the vendor receipt. Wipe the rest, then burn the controller. → Destruction exit.
- **V, publish:** Publish the rules. Strip the people out. **Velvet:** Redacted export, signature check, revoke the import account. In that order. → Publication exit.

On interruption: **Velvet:** Not committed. We still have the local copy. Finish the check before we send or erase anything. → Retry same transaction, not a second choice payout.
