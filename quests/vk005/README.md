# VK005 — Crash Test Sweetheart

Racing / sabotage / action. Target: 25–35 minutes. First draft; not implemented. [Dialogue](script/dialogue.md) · [Implementation](plan.md).

Niko transported clinic equipment and is owed a settlement held by an underground race organizer. Its ledger can identify who bought Sable's archived recordings. His proposed exchange: enter the race in his second car, collect its entry-bound ledger token and split the purse. The organizer issued participant tokens before the sabotage began; tracing the broker does not require winning. Prize money buys a clean archive reader, but Velvet can fund one herself after a loss. No driving-performance gate blocks VK006.

Velvet has modified an autonomous compact with an ordinary manual override. V drives; she occupies the passenger seat, watching telemetry and tracing unauthorized race-control commands. The car remains under the player's control: scripted sabotage is confined to NPC competitors until a reviewed player-vehicle control proof exists. Explain the override before the countdown rather than blaming control bugs on the story.

At staging, V meets Niko, inspects the supplied car and agrees on the race rules. Velvet's commentary changes with actual checkpoint progress, collisions and position where those events are available; unsupported telemetry is cut instead of inventing reactions. A quiet pre-grid beat lets V ask why she agreed to leave her bench. She wanted a problem that could be seen through a windscreen, and is about to regret the distinction.

Mid-course, Niko's vehicle receives a brake-lock command and crashes into a surveyed safe pull-off. V gets a clear announced choice at a route fork: continue through the next gate, or stop at the rescue marker. Nobody dies because V spent time reading a dialogue wheel; the decision is spatial and announced before the junction. Rescue retires V's race entry cleanly and becomes an on-foot scene. Continuing retains the race and lets Velvet send Niko an emergency call; his condition is reported later rather than silently decided by a hidden timer.

The saboteur, the organizer's collection agent, is forcing entrants into default to seize their vehicles. After rescue or finish, the ledger token points to a physical control relay near the paddock. V can expose the signed commands, disable the relay or fight its guard. The organizer's local collection operation is shut down and Niko's ledger record copied. The memory reseller's identity leads into VK006.

Rescuing Niko guarantees his safe exit and costs the win. Continuing can yield a real win or loss; Velvet respects V's driving but is clear about the choice to pass him. The quest does not conceal a full prize behind a secret rescue route. Both paths pay the guaranteed job fee; only a recorded win grants the prize bonus. Losing does not erase a completed investigation.

Guaranteed proposed fee: 8,000 eddies. Genuine win bonus: 4,000. Rescue keepsake: Niko's broken/repaired race token. Win keepsake: a ribbon Velvet calls insufficiently black. New reader access is a story service, not a silently granted money sink.
